BINARY SEARCH TREES
- A binary search tree can only have at most two children
Binary Search Trees Have: 
- a right child
- a left child
- a right subtree
- a left subtree

The right child is always greater than its parent
the left child is always less than its parent
The right subtree is always greater than the root
The left subtree is always less than the root 
the right and left subtree are binary search trees 

FULL BINARY TREE: every node in the tree has two children
if T is not empty and has height h > 0, T is a full binary tree if 
both its roots subtrees are full binary trees of height h - 1
EQUATION: h >= 0 has 2^h - 1 (Maximum number of nodes)

COMPLETE BINARY TREE: a binary tree that is full down to level h-1, 
with level h filled in from left to right
1. all nodes at level h-2 and above have two children
2. nodes at level h-1 has children, all same level left nodes have two children
3. node at level h-1 has one child that is a left child

FULLY BALANCED: If the height of any node’s right subtree differs
from the height of the node’s left subtree by no more than 1
